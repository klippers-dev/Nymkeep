Nymkeep development candidate

The following major components are used by Nymkeep. Full upstream terms and
runtime notices are in licenses/. The target-specific DEPENDENCIES inventory
preserves resolved Rust/production JavaScript license texts and copyrights.
Review native bundled libraries and vendor redistribution terms before publication.

Linux GLib 0.18.5: MIT, upstream license included in DEPENDENCIES.txt.
The shipped source backports gtk-rs/gtk-rs-core PR #1343 for RUSTSEC-2024-0429.
Original archive hashes and the exact two-line fix are recorded in
vendor/glib-backport.json in the public Nymkeep source repository.
https://github.com/klippers-dev/Nymkeep

Windows OCR, Microsoft Edge WebView2 and macOS Vision/Accessibility are vendor

redistributable runtime DLLs, copyright Microsoft Corporation, under the
Visual Studio redistributable terms. Their signatures/hashes are checked at build time.

No signing keys, passwords, customer data or license credentials are bundled.
