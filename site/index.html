A few things
worth knowing.

Privacy tools should be easy to understand.

Talk to the team
Can it mask screenshots and images?

Yes, screenshot mode is built and verified in the Windows development app. It uses local OCR to suggest sensitive text, supports manual boxes even without OCR, previews the actual redaction, and lets you copy the image or save a PNG. Selected pixels become solid black. Faces and objects are not detected automatically. Image pixels cannot be restored. The website's image example is fixed fiction, not an upload tool.

Does Nymkeep upload my text?

Protect and Restore run on your PC, including detection and the session map. Those operations do not send your text over the network. When you paste the reviewed version into an AI service, that service receives what you paste.

Can it catch every sensitive detail?

No. Detection is assistive, and names can be missed. Review the result, protect missed phrases and use custom terms where needed. Reversible placeholders are pseudonymization, not guaranteed anonymity.

How do I get my original details back?

Copy the AI reply and use Restore in the same app session. Known placeholders return to their original values. Unknown placeholders stay unchanged for review. Mappings expire after 30 minutes without use; quitting ends the session.

Which apps and platforms does it work with?

Windows is the first implementation target. Selection capture works where apps expose selected text; other apps use a labeled clipboard fallback. macOS, Linux and Wayland support still need implementation and real-device verification.

Is the Windows download available yet?

The public installer is not available yet. Signed packaging, real-device checks and a privacy review come before the first public release. The download will appear in the release section above.

What should I know about the clipboard?

Protect copies the protected version. Restore can put original sensitive details back on the clipboard. Your operating system's clipboard history or sync may retain copied text. Clear that history when appropriate for your work.
