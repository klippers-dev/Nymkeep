# Community code of conduct

Contributors, maintainers and participants should be respectful, clear and
constructive. Critique technical work and evidence, and make room for different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, impersonation, deliberate disruption
and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Use
synthetic examples in reports and follow `SECURITY.md` for sensitive findings.

Report conduct concerns privately to **contact.klippers@gmail.com**. Include only
the information needed to understand the concern. Maintainers handle reports
discreetly and may edit/remove content, close a discussion, warn a participant or
restrict participation based on severity and repetition. Actions can be appealed
through the same address with new context. This policy applies to project issues,
PRs, discussions and other official project spaces.
