# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Desktop users on Windows 11 first (then macOS/Linux) who paste text into AI tools all day: developers, analysts, support and sales people, consultants. They work across browsers, IDEs, email, terminals, CRMs, and chat clients. The job: use any AI with real work text without exposing raw sensitive content, in seconds, without switching windows.

## Product Purpose

Nymkeep is a shortcut-first, local-first privacy utility. Select text anywhere, press Protect & Copy, and Nymkeep replaces sensitive details locally with reversible aliases (`PERSON_1`, `EMAIL_1`...) on the clipboard. Paste into any AI. Copy the answer, press Restore, and originals return on-device. Success means Protect and Restore feel instant, work in every text app, stay under 200 MB, and never send raw text anywhere.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, trustworthy. Three words: discreet, exact, reliable. It should feel like a system utility that has always been there, not a startup landing page.

## Anti-references

- Enterprise DLP dashboards, compliance-suite chrome, admin-console density
- Marketing-site styling inside the app (heroes, gradient CTAs, eyebrow labels)
- Noisy security products that alarm on every match

## Design Principles

1. Shortcut-first: the full window is a fallback; tray + toast are the primary surface.
2. Never pretend certainty: show counts and confidence, support review, never claim perfect detection.
3. Invisible until invoked: cold start under 1.5 s, idle RAM under 100 MB, zero network during protect/restore.
4. Reversible by contract: stable tokens, visible mapping, deterministic restore.
5. Honesty about fallbacks: clipboard mode is labeled and warned about, never silently presented as secure capture.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Full keyboard access for review panel and settings. Reduced-motion alternatives for toasts. No color-only status indicators. Never display raw PII in toasts.
